Every difference is less than 0.01 percentage points. The remaining ten parameters were reoptimized; this tests the formulation's predictive sensitivity, not invariance of individual parameter estimates. It does not prove the absence of lactate inhibition biologically. The original model is retained while practical-identifiability questions are resolved.
