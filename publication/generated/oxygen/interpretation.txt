The 500 mL result is a scaling scenario anchored to the Neto 100 mL correlation. It is not a Neto correlation prediction for 500 mL or an experimental measurement. Actual surface CFD and vessel geometry have not verified these scaling assumptions. The intra-aggregate calculation uses assumed packing and diffusivity, uniform consumption and surface concentration equal to bulk. Negative concentrations invalidate that steady-state regime. A 336 um mean diameter is not the maximum individual diameter. Do not claim that hypoxia is excluded.
