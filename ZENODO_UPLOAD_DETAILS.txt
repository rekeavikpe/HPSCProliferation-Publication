TITLE
Code, data and cached results for In Silico Modeling of Shear Stress and Energy Dissipation Rate Effects on Human Pluripotent Stem Cell Proliferation in Vertical-Wheel Bioreactors

DESCRIPTION
Research code, curated measurements and CFD exposure summaries, saved model fits, final likelihood profiles, predictions, and figure/table reproduction scripts supporting the accepted Stem Cells Translational Medicine article SCTM-26-0107.R2. Code is Apache-2.0; data, cached results and figures are CC-BY-4.0. The archive does not contain raw CFD simulation projects or all historical optimization checkpoints. See README.md and LICENSES.md.

The release uses the same model equations, calibration/selection split and selected parameters as the final manuscript. It includes code, curated measurements, CFD summary inputs, selected-fit caches, final profile likelihoods and scripts for numerical checks and figure/table reproduction. It starts from exported CFD summaries; original CFD projects, particle trajectory exports and every historical optimizer checkpoint are not included. Two coauthor-prepared figures are supplied as reference artwork. Third-party reference inputs retain their original terms and are fetched separately where required.
